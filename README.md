# Feng Shui Advisory App 🏡✨

A comprehensive web application that provides personalized Feng Shui analysis and recommendations for room configurations. Users can input their space details, direction, location, and personal preferences to receive expert guidance on harmonizing their environment according to traditional Feng Shui principles.

## 🌟 Features

### Room Configuration Analysis
- **Room Type Selection**: Bedroom, Living Room, Kitchen, Office, Dining Room, Bathroom, Entryway
- **Direction & Location**: 8 directional orientations with Bagua map integration
- **Space Configuration**: Door position, window count, room size, floor level
- **Natural Light Analysis**: Multiple light source detection and optimization

### Five Elements Integration
- **Wood Element**: Growth, creativity, flexibility
- **Fire Element**: Energy, passion, recognition  
- **Earth Element**: Stability, grounding, support
- **Metal Element**: Precision, efficiency, clarity
- **Water Element**: Flow, wisdom, career success

### Color Harmony Analysis
- Interactive color selection with gradient backgrounds
- Element-color correspondence analysis
- Room-specific color recommendations
- Bagua area color alignment

### Personalized Recommendations
- **Birth Year Analysis**: Personal element calculation based on Chinese astrology
- **Purpose-Driven Advice**: Customized suggestions for relaxation, productivity, creativity, wealth, relationships, or health
- **Directional Guidance**: Bagua map integration for specific life aspects

### Comprehensive Scoring System
- Overall Feng Shui score (0-100)
- Detailed breakdown of strengths and areas for improvement
- Visual score indicators with color-coded categories
- Specific enhancement suggestions

## 📋 How to Use

1. **Basic Information**
   - Select your room type from the dropdown
   - Enter the room size in square feet

2. **Location & Direction**
   - Choose the main entry direction (8 compass directions)
   - Select floor level and natural light sources

3. **Room Configuration**
   - Specify door position and window count
   - Select current elements present in the room

4. **Color Scheme**
   - Pick the dominant color in your space
   - Visual color selection with gradient backgrounds

5. **Personal Information** (Optional)
   - Enter birth year for personal element analysis
   - Choose primary purpose for the space

6. **Get Analysis**
   - Click "Get Feng Shui Analysis" to receive recommendations
   - Review detailed insights and suggestions
   - Use "New Analysis" button to start over

## 🔮 Feng Shui Principles Implemented

### Bagua Map Integration
- **North**: Career & Life Path (Water element)
- **Northeast**: Knowledge & Self-Cultivation (Earth element)
- **East**: Health & Family (Wood element)
- **Southeast**: Wealth & Prosperity (Wood element)
- **South**: Fame & Reputation (Fire element)
- **Southwest**: Love & Marriage (Earth element)
- **West**: Children & Creativity (Metal element)
- **Northwest**: Helpful People & Travel (Metal element)

### Five Elements Theory
Complete implementation of the five elements cycle:
- **Generation Cycle**: Wood feeds Fire, Fire creates Earth, Earth bears Metal, Metal holds Water, Water nourishes Wood
- **Destruction Cycle**: Wood depletes Earth, Earth absorbs Water, Water extinguishes Fire, Fire melts Metal, Metal cuts Wood

### Room-Specific Guidelines
Each room type has tailored recommendations:
- **Bedrooms**: Focus on rest, romance, and personal energy
- **Kitchens**: Balance fire and water elements, avoid conflicting energies
- **Offices**: Enhance career and knowledge areas
- **Living Rooms**: Create gathering and social harmony
- **Bathrooms**: Manage water energy and prevent drain effects
